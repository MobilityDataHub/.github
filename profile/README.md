# MobilityDataHub

### Share data. Reuse methods. Understand results.

**MobilityDataHub is an open and federated digital commons designed to help territories observe, understand and support the decarbonisation of mobility.**

It aims to facilitate the reuse of **mobility data, analytical methods, software components, indicators and applications**, while preserving the autonomy of territories and data providers.

MobilityDataHub is developed around a simple principle:

> **Centralise the discovery and understanding of resources, not necessarily their storage.**

---

## 🎯 Why MobilityDataHub?

Mobility data is increasingly available, but turning it into actionable knowledge remains complex.

Data is distributed across many providers and platforms. Analytical methods are often redeveloped for individual studies. Indicators with similar names may rely on different sources, methodologies or geographical scopes. As a result, knowledge and tools developed for one territory are still difficult to reuse elsewhere.

MobilityDataHub aims to provide a shared framework to:

- discover and access mobility data;
- reuse documented analytical methods and software components;
- define and calculate transparent mobility indicators;
- compare results across time and territories when relevant;
- trace how results were produced;
- share tools and knowledge between territories and mobility stakeholders.

---

## 🧩 More than a data catalogue

MobilityDataHub is built around connected resources rather than isolated datasets.

### Data

Mobility datasets from public authorities, transport operators, research projects, open-data portals and other providers.

### Methods & reusable components

Data preparation, quality control, harmonisation, modelling, simulation, calculation and visualisation components.

### Indicators

Documented mobility indicators including their definition, unit, calculation method, scope and conditions of comparability.

### Applications

Observatories, analytical tools and simulation applications combining data and reusable components to address operational or policy needs.

---

## 🔗 Lineage and reproducibility

A key objective of MobilityDataHub is to make the production of results understandable and traceable.

A result should progressively be linked to:

**Data → Processing / Method → Indicator → Result → Application**

The lineage model aims to document:

- input datasets and their versions;
- methods and software versions;
- parameters and assumptions;
- geographical and temporal scope;
- produced outputs;
- relationships between resources.

This approach makes it easier to understand, update, audit and reproduce mobility analyses.

---

## 🌐 An open and federated architecture

MobilityDataHub does **not** require all mobility data to be hosted in a single infrastructure.

Resources may remain hosted:

- by their original producer;
- on public open-data portals;
- in project-specific infrastructures;
- in CKAN instances;
- in data spaces;
- in territorial or partner infrastructures.

MobilityDataHub provides a common layer for **discovery, documentation, interoperability and reuse**.

> **One shared reference framework, open interfaces, multiple hosting and execution environments.**

---

## 🏙️ Built for territories

MobilityDataHub aims to help territories reuse existing resources instead of rebuilding complete analysis chains for every study.

Typical use cases include:

- territorial mobility diagnostics;
- monitoring mobility trends;
- calculation of mobility and decarbonisation indicators;
- evaluation of transport policies;
- comparison of scenarios;
- infrastructure impact assessment;
- monitoring of mobility objectives over time.

The **Services express régionaux métropolitains (SERM)** provide a first opportunity to industrialise this approach across several French territories.

---

## 🧪 From research to operational use

MobilityDataHub builds on research and development activities carried out by **IFPEN, Cerema and Inria**, including work initiated within the **PEPR MOBIDEC / Mob Sci-Dat Factory** project.

The objective is now to move progressively from experimental components to reusable and maintainable services that can support territorial mobility projects at scale.

---

## 🏗️ Architecture principles

MobilityDataHub follows several core principles:

**Open standards**  
Use existing standards whenever possible for metadata, software description and provenance.

**Interoperability**  
Components should communicate through documented and versioned interfaces.

**Federation**  
Data does not have to be moved to MobilityDataHub to become discoverable and reusable.

**Modularity**  
Storage, cataloguing, computation and applications should remain loosely coupled.

**Traceability**  
Results should be linked to the data, methods and assumptions used to produce them.

**Reusability**  
Methods should be designed to be applied to several territories whenever possible.

**Open source**  
Generic components and specifications should be open whenever legal, contractual and intellectual-property constraints allow it.

## 🤝 An ecosystem rather than a single platform

MobilityDataHub is intended to interact with existing data infrastructures rather than replace them.

Open-data platforms, CKAN instances, data spaces, territorial infrastructures and third-party services can all contribute to the ecosystem through interoperable interfaces.

The long-term objective is to make **methods, indicators and knowledge reusable regardless of where the underlying data is hosted**.

---

## 🌱 Contributing

MobilityDataHub is intended to become a contributive ecosystem.

Potential contributors include:

- local and regional authorities;
- transport authorities and operators;
- research organisations;
- data providers;
- engineering and consulting companies;
- software and digital infrastructure providers;
- mobility researchers and developers.

Contributions may concern datasets, specifications, connectors, analytical methods, software components, indicators, documentation or use cases.

Contribution guidelines and governance rules will be published as the initiative develops.

---

## 🧭 Long-term ambition

MobilityDataHub aims to become a shared reference point for mobility data, methods and indicators.

Its success will not be measured by the amount of data stored in a central infrastructure, but by the ability of the mobility community to:

**reuse existing resources, share methodologies, reproduce analyses and collectively build better knowledge about mobility and its decarbonisation.**
